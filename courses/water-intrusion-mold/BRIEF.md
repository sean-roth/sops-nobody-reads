# Brief — Water Intrusion & Mold Prevention

**Audience:** apartment maintenance technicians. **Buyer:** property managers.
**Source:** EPA 402-K-01-001, *Mold Remediation in Schools and Commercial Buildings* (public domain). Guidance, not regulation — framed as "the standard playbook."

## The belief flip
- **From:** mold is a cleaning problem that happens to you.
- **To:** mold is the predictable output of a moisture system you manage. *Own the loops, not the leaks.*

## Stakes
Expensive and creeping, **not fatal** — property damage, cost, reputation, tenant health, liability. (Inverse of the LOTO course's risk profile: no body count. The dread is entropy — the building's slow death and the home-as-sanctuary — not mortality.)

## Thematic frame
Mold as the ancient decomposer that makes life possible — "without malice or judgment." The calm naturalist narrator is the horror. In the wild it's the cleanup crew; in a home we call it rot.

## What each module must deliver
- **M1** — reframe mold as a moisture-following decomposer; establish the 48-hour clock and the bet (control the moisture).
- **M2** — the four loops that catch water in time: the walk, the tenants, the work orders, the turnovers.
- **M3** — the first hours once water is found: find the source, respond inside the window, and know when to escalate to a professional.

## Key source anchors
- **Table 1** — 24–48 hr water-damage first response.
- **Table 2** — remediation by affected area (small area = maintenance; large area = professional). Thresholds are guidance; the client's real numbers/policy must be set before any client version ships.
