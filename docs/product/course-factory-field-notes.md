## 2026-07-08 — The Phase 2 persona has a face: the internal training coordinator

Source: live cold call, SMB manufacturer segment, Chicago metro. A shop large enough to have a dedicated internal training person; she answered the training question herself and read the offer as a threat to her role.

**The insight:** the self-service app's user was previously a vague "professional." The field says it's her — the internal training coordinator at companies just above the current service segment's size ceiling. And she is not merely the customer: **she is the accountable signer the Durability Standard's gate architecture assumes.** Source acceptance, scope rulings, conflict resolution, attestation — in self-service, those gates are her job, elevated. The governing principle (the system never makes decisions; it forces them to the decision-maker with perfect information) makes her structurally non-eliminable: the app removes slide labor, not judgment.

**Secondary insight — she solves elicitation access.** The founder's elicitation is remote-only by rule (elicitor stays out of the source). An internal coordinator has no such constraint: she knows who holds tribal knowledge and can run the interview protocol as a skill from inside the building. In self-service, elicitation likely ships as a skill she operates.

**Positioning problem identified early:** the market's default frame is binary — replaces-me / doesn't-replace-me. The app's messaging must break the binary before it forms: *the slides were never the job; the judgment was.* Field evidence: first contact with the persona bristled. Expect that reaction; design the first-touch experience against it.

**Market segmentation implied:** no internal training person (SMB, owner answers the phone) → service client, now. Internal training person exists → app customer, later. The boundary sits roughly at the current segment's employee-count ceiling.

**Status:** noted for Phase 2. No design work authorized; Phase 2 trigger unchanged (first client-must-click-Approve moment, per architecture direction v0.2).
